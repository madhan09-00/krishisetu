Problem Statement SIH26033 Evaluation Brief

🎯 Problem Addressed

Multiple intermediaries (commission agents, aggregators, wholesalers) capture 60–70% of the consumer rupee while farmers get distress prices and consumers pay inflated rates.

1. Multilingual Voice AI Non-tech-savvy farmers list produce in native regional dialects via speech synthesis & entity parsing.
2. Edge Computer Vision Automated crop defect, size, and ripeness grading giving buyers guaranteed quality certificates without middlemen.
3. Shared Load Pooling Geospatial aggregation of smallholder yields to cut freight cost per kg by up to 45%.
